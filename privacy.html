Anvil Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026 · Applies to Anvil for Android (com.chris.anvil)

The short version
  • Anvil is a text and code editor. It has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no developer servers.
  • Your files, passwords, tokens, API keys and AI prompts never pass through the developer. They go only from your device to the services you choose to connect.
  • Passwords, tokens and API keys are stored encrypted on your device using the Android Keystore.
  • Crash reports stay on your device unless you choose to share one.
  • The developer only receives what you choose to send: support emails, AI response reports and crash reports.

1. Who we are

Anvil is made by Chris Mitchell, an individual developer based in Glasgow, Scotland, United Kingdom ("I", "we" or "the developer"). For the limited personal data described in section 8, I am the data controller under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, and, where it applies, the EU General Data Protection Regulation.

Contact:

I do not receive or have access to your files, credentials or AI conversations, so I am not a controller or processor of that data. When you connect Anvil to a server or service, that service handles your data under its own terms and privacy policy.

2. The two versions of Anvil

Anvil is distributed in two versions. This policy covers both, and points out where they differ.

  • Google Play version. Opens files through the Android file picker and the folders you grant it. It does not request "All files access" and does not include Google Drive integration or Google sign-in.
  • Full version, downloaded from GitHub. Includes everything in the Google Play version, plus optional "All files access" (so you can browse device storage directly) and optional Google Drive integration using Google sign-in.

3. What Anvil does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no access to your advertising ID.
  • No automatic crash reporting.
  • No developer-operated servers or accounts. There is no "Anvil account".
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or calendar.
  • No background network activity. Anvil connects to the internet only when you use a feature that needs it.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data for marketing.

4. Where your data goes when you use a feature

Files on your device

Files you open from your device stay on your device. Anvil reads and writes them only where you direct it, through the Android file picker, folders you grant access to or, in the full version, All files access if you turn it on. If your device is rooted and you turn on root mode, Anvil can read and write system locations you choose.

Servers you configure (SFTP, FTP/FTPS, SMB, WebDAV)

When you add a remote location, Anvil connects directly from your device to the server address you enter. It sends your username and password or private key, the folder and file names you browse, and the contents of files you open or save. The server's owner (which may be you, your employer or a hosting company) controls what happens to that data.

Plain FTP and http:// WebDAV addresses are not encrypted. Anyone on the network path could read your password and files. Use SFTP, FTPS (TLS) or https:// wherever you can.

GitHub

If you connect a GitHub repository, Anvil talks directly to GitHub (or to your GitHub Enterprise server) using the personal access token you provide. It sends the repository, branch and file paths you work with, file contents you save, and commit messages you write. Commits are made as the owner of the token. GitHub's handling of that data is covered by the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Google Drive and Google sign-in (full version only)

The full version can open and save files in your Google Drive. When you choose "Connect Google Drive", Google asks you to sign in and to allow Anvil to see, edit, create and delete files in your Drive. Google may also share your basic profile and email address with the app as part of sign-in.

How Anvil uses this access:

  • Anvil uses your Google data only to show your Drive folders and to open, save, create, rename and delete the files you act on.
  • All requests go directly from your device to Google. Your Google data, access tokens and files are never sent to the developer or any other third party.
  • Anvil does not use Google data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not use it to train AI models.
  • Nobody (including the developer) reads your Drive data. It is only displayed to you on your device.
  • Anvil keeps the signed-in account in the app's private storage so you stay connected. Google may cache sign-in details on your device under its own terms.

Anvil's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To disconnect, sign out of Google Drive in Anvil. To remove Anvil's access to your Google Account completely, visit myaccount.google.com/connections. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

The Google Play version does not include Google Drive or Google sign-in. You can still open Drive files there through the standard Android file picker, which is handled by the Google Drive app, not by Anvil.

AI assistant

The AI assistant is optional and off until you add your own API key from one of the supported providers. When you use it, Anvil sends your request directly from your device to the provider you selected, authenticated with your key. The developer never sees the request or the reply. Before your first request, Anvil shows a one-time notice explaining this.

Each request contains:

  • your question, or the quick action you picked (for example "Explain");
  • depending on the scope you choose: the selected text, the whole current document (up to 60,000 characters) or nothing;
  • the document's file name (not its full path) and the name of its programming language;
  • your API key, in the request header.

Replies are shown in the app. Anvil does not keep a history of your AI conversations.

Your provider processes this data under your own agreement with them. That agreement decides how long they keep it and whether they may use it to improve their models; free tiers often allow more use than paid ones. Check before sending confidential material:

  • OpenAI: Privacy Policy and API data usage
  • Anthropic: Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini: Google Privacy Policy and Gemini API Additional Terms

Report response. If an AI reply is harmful or offensive, you can tap "Report response". This opens your email app with a message to the developer containing the prompt and the reply. Nothing is sent until you review the email and press send yourself. See section 8.

Voice dictation

Voice dictation uses your device's speech recogniser or keyboard (for example Gboard). Anvil does not record audio; it only receives the recognised text. The recogniser or keyboard handles your voice under its own privacy policy.

Previews, printing and sharing

  • Markdown and HTML previews do not load anything from the internet, so remote images and trackers in a document are not fetched. Tapping a link opens it in your browser.
  • Printing sends the document to the Android print service you choose.
  • Sharing sends content to the app you pick in the Android share sheet.

6. Android backup

Android can back up app data to your Google account and copy it to a new device. Anvil excludes the following from backup: your session (including unsaved text), local history, crash reports and all stored passwords, tokens and API keys. Other settings may be included. Android backups are handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, and you can turn backup off in Android Settings.

7. Crash reports

If Anvil crashes or recovers from an error, it saves a report on your device. A report contains the app version, Android version, device manufacturer and model, memory use and a technical stack trace. The stack trace may include file names, folder paths, server names or usernames. It does not deliberately include the contents of your documents.

Reports are never sent automatically. You can view, copy or share them from Settings → Diagnostics. Read a report before you share it, and remove anything you don't want to send.

9. International transfers

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and GitHub are based in the United States and may process data there or elsewhere. When you use the AI assistant, GitHub or Google Drive, you send data to them directly under your own agreement with them; their privacy policies explain how they protect international transfers.

My email provider may store support emails outside the UK. Where it does, I rely on safeguards recognised under UK data protection law, such as UK adequacy regulations (including the UK–US data bridge for certified companies) or the International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

10. Security

  • Passwords, keys and tokens are encrypted on your device with a key held in the Android Keystore, and are sent only to the service they belong to.
  • Connections to AI providers, GitHub and Google use HTTPS.
  • SFTP remembers each server's fingerprint and warns you if it changes.
  • Plain FTP and http:// connections you choose to use are not encrypted.

No system is perfectly secure. Keep your device locked and updated, and revoke tokens or keys you no longer need with the service that issued them.

11. Children

Anvil is a tool for developers and writers. It is not directed at children, and I do not knowingly receive personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent me personal data, contact me and I will delete it.

12. Your rights

Under UK GDPR (and EU GDPR if you are in the EU or EEA) you have the right to:

  • get a copy of the personal data I hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict or object to how I use it;
  • receive your data in a portable format, where that applies.

To use any of these rights, email chris.mitchell@cjmprojects.co.uk. I will reply within one month. If your request is complex I may need up to two more months, and I will tell you if so. I may ask you to confirm your identity first.

Because I don't hold your files, credentials or AI conversations, I can't access or delete them. To delete that data, use the controls in Anvil (section 5) or contact the service concerned (your server, GitHub, Google or your AI provider).
